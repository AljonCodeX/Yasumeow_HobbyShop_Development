Before you sign up

Do I need to bring my own gear?

For Beyblade and Tamiya events you can bring your own build, but we keep loaners and starter sets on hand. For card games, bring your deck. We sell sleeves and supplies at the counter.

How do I pay the entry fee?

Pay in-store or via GCash on the day. We confirm your slot once it's locked.

How are prizes decided?

Prizes depend on how many players join. The more players, the bigger the prize pool. Some events give cards as prizes.
